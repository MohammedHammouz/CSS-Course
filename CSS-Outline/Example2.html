4) Observations

  • The outline is drawn outside the border and does not affect the box model or element dimensions.
  • Unlike borders, outlines do not push or resize adjacent elements—they sit “on top” of the layout.
  • Outlines can overlap neighboring content if the offset is negative (not shown) or minimal.
  • Outlines are not limited by border radius and can follow non-rectangular element shapes in some browsers.
